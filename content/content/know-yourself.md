> [!TLDR]
> - Before you write posts, get interviewed (by a person or by AI) and keep the transcript as your brand document.
> - Then compile what makes your favourite writers distinctive into a second document.
> - Feed both to AI every time you ideate or draft, so the output sounds like you.

## Write the brand document first

Dan Koe's process for a new client starts with a recorded interview. The transcript becomes the brand document, and it goes into the AI every time anyone plans or drafts a post.

You can do this alone. Paste the questions below into Claude, have it interview you one section at a time, and save the answers in one file. Exact words matter more than polish: the way your reader complains to a friend is the language your posts should use.

> [!TRICK] Answer the nine-section brand interview
> 1. Who you help: Describe one real person. What does a bad day look like for them? What do they say when they complain to a friend (exact words)? What have they already tried that didn't work?
> 2. Your story: What have you lived through that your audience is living through now? The moment things changed? What have you built, tried, failed at, figured out (biggest to smallest)? What results can you point to?
> 3. Who shaped your mind: Whose books, posts, videos do you never skip (real names and titles)? Which idea do you keep coming back to and why? Who changed how you think, even if you now disagree?
> 4. Pillars and perspective: What could you talk about for hours (everything)? Which 2-3 matter most? How do you come at each differently ("through psychology", "as a former nurse")? What do people ask your advice on? What do you keep explaining?
> 5. Mission and beliefs: One sentence on what you help people do. What do you believe that most in your space don't? What common advice is wrong?
> 6. What you're against: What way of living hurts your people? What life do you help them escape? What myth or habit (not a person or company) would you remove?
> 7. Why now: What just changed in the world that most haven't noticed?
> 8. Where you're taking them: Their day-to-day five years after following you? What would they proudly put in their bio? Who are you in their story (guide, documenter, someone who got out and came back with a map)?
> 9. Rules and words: 5-12 rules your ideal reader lives by. Names for your own ideas, methods or problems, with meanings.

## Study the voices that shaped you

Don't try to clone anyone's voice with AI. Instead, **write down what makes a voice distinctive**: the core ideas, the turns of phrase with personality, the worldview people rally behind. That document makes drafting easier without making you a copy.

Run it on the people you learn from. Also read their top posts on each platform yourself and note the patterns that repeat. If you're not posting yet, list 5 to 10 people who shaped your thinking. Your angle is how you combine and reinterpret them.

> [!TRICK] Extract a voice-and-ideas document
> Give an AI that can read social posts the handles and run this prompt. Koe connects Eden (Koe's own tool) to Claude or ChatGPT over MCP; any assistant with web access works, or paste the posts in yourself.
>
> ```
> Pull the best content from [@handles]. 10-20 posts from each platform they are on. Then, I want you to note down the core ideas that make them unique, the aspects of their voice that have personality, and a guess at what their worldview or philosophy is that could rally other people behind it. In essence, note down everything that led to their growth, and compile it into a document.
> ```
>
> For people who don't post much, ask the AI to pull their best ideas from their books and the web instead.

## Keep both documents close

These two files are the context every later step reads: the content map in *Plan six months of ideas in one sitting*, and any AI editor you build in *Build a voice nobody can copy*. Update them when your story changes.

A note on evidence: this is Koe's client process, described anecdotally, and Koe founded Eden, the tool the article recommends. The questions and prompt work in any assistant.
