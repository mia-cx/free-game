Major marketing missteps I have observed in Web3 

A long 🧵

### Thread 1 @kunalvg (Mon Jul 31 05:47:40 +0000 2023, 4 likes, 0 bookmarks)

1) Undefined Positioning leads to:

- Poor product-market fit 

This stems from not being obsessed enough with users. 

- Poor Go To Market strategy: 
Stems from not putting enough brains in- ICP, Acquisition channels, and How to scale acquisitions channels.

### Thread 2 @kunalvg (Mon Jul 31 05:47:41 +0000 2023, 3 likes, 0 bookmarks)

2) Poor strategy 

Grear strategy triumphs great execution. Most have mediocre strategy.

Execution matters, but execution with the right strategy matters more.

### Thread 3 @kunalvg (Mon Jul 31 05:47:41 +0000 2023, 3 likes, 1 bookmarks)

3) Being overly obsessed with the community than the product.

A great community is a result of a great product. 

Not the other way around.

### Thread 4 @kunalvg (Mon Jul 31 05:47:42 +0000 2023, 4 likes, 0 bookmarks)

4) Not taking a product marketing approach.

Acquisition-&gt; Onboarding-&gt; Engagement-&gt; Retention-&gt; Monetization

Most Web3 projects should start with a smoother onboarding experience.

### Thread 5 @kunalvg (Mon Jul 31 05:47:42 +0000 2023, 3 likes, 1 bookmarks)

5) Ignoring the personal branding of founders

Founder's presence and participation on crypto Twitter matters. 

Igniting personal brands fuels project success.

### Thread 6 @kunalvg (Mon Jul 31 05:47:43 +0000 2023, 3 likes, 0 bookmarks)

6) Content marketing blunders 

- Not pushing high-quality content consistently

- Missing thunderclap (popular narratives) content opportunities

- Not having a content distribution strategy

- Making it hard for users to comprehend your content

### Thread 7 @kunalvg (Mon Jul 31 05:47:43 +0000 2023, 4 likes, 0 bookmarks)

7) Not being smart about using influencers

Mediocre influencers can bring huge numbers. But that won't manifest into anything substantial for the project. 

Instead, use quality influencers to build the right narrative and attract the right audience.

### Thread 8 @kunalvg (Mon Jul 31 05:47:44 +0000 2023, 4 likes, 0 bookmarks)

8) Ignoring SEO

Can't emphasize this enough. 

SEO is the most rewarding content distribution strategy. 

There is a huge opportunity here for Web3 projects as many high-search volume Web3 keywords are not catered to with quality content.

### Thread 9 @kunalvg (Mon Jul 31 05:47:44 +0000 2023, 5 likes, 0 bookmarks)

9) Not treating launches as micro GTM and ignoring the importance of videos in them.

@thirdweb did it really well with their launch.

### Thread 10 @kunalvg (Mon Jul 31 05:47:44 +0000 2023, 3 likes, 0 bookmarks)

10) Not having a wider TG 

This stems from a lack of real-world use cases. 

Though some say we are in the infra era, consumer apps are as important.
