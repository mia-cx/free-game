Web3 marketing tactic: conversations as content marketing (CACM)

This stems from the clutter of all the preachy content that most brands/ protocols produce, making it hard to stand out or gain benefits.

In CACM, you try to evoke conversations with all stakeholders in and around your ecosystem.

Whatever you generate out of these conversations, you repurpose all that.

Ex. You do a 5-minute conversation with a user. Record it. Post it as a video. Convert this video into a tweet thread. Convert the tweet thread into a LinkedIn post. Take a central idea from the conversation and create more tweets.

If you can crack a bigger network brand/ individual to converse with you, it would immensely help your brand, too.
