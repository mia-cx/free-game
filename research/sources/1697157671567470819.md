### Thread 1 @kunalvg (Thu Aug 31 08:01:35 +0000 2023, 7 likes, 0 bookmarks)

Build an Effective Marketing Framework:

Starting marketing practice from scratch is both fun &amp; challenging.

Begin by aligning objectives with stakeholders. Create a detailed plan for the next 3 months with achievable KPIs.

Break it into weekly action items for maximum impact.

### Thread 2 @kunalvg (Thu Aug 31 08:01:36 +0000 2023, 5 likes, 0 bookmarks)

Macro-Micro Goals:

The best way to set KPIs, specifically for Digital Marketing, is to divide them into Macro and Micro KPIs. 

Example:
Macro: Improve the bounce rate by 10% over 3 months

Micro: Write 3 engaging articles every month, getting 'X' amount of traffic every month

### Thread 3 @kunalvg (Thu Aug 31 08:01:36 +0000 2023, 7 likes, 1 bookmarks)

Cultivate a Writing Culture:

For tech companies, niche content is gold. 

Foster a writing culture. 

Encourage your team to document the problems they've solved. 

We did this, resulting in 70+ articles over four years, driving substantial traffic and business.

### Thread 4 @kunalvg (Thu Aug 31 08:01:37 +0000 2023, 7 likes, 0 bookmarks)

Report Consistently:

Demonstrate progress by generating custom weekly and monthly reports of marketing activities. 

Leverage Google Analytics and Search Console for insights. 

My comprehensive reports gave stakeholders a clear, evolving view of our performance.

### Thread 5 @kunalvg (Thu Aug 31 08:01:37 +0000 2023, 7 likes, 0 bookmarks)

SEO Is All About Content; everything else is secondary:

Even without extensive link-building efforts, our articles secured top SERP rankings.

Dive into keyword research guidelines for effective content creation. All standard content marketing principles apply here for success!

### Thread 6 @kunalvg (Thu Aug 31 08:01:38 +0000 2023, 7 likes, 0 bookmarks)

Employees's Digital Presence Energises Brand's Digital Presence:

I pushed to make social media training a part of the HR orientation for new employees.

The first person I trained in our organization was our CEO. 

We achieved a lot more through social media than we thought.

Understand The Industry and Ecosystem:

I spent much time reading and talking to experts around me to understand Big Data and AI.

Except for actual coding, I went as deep as possible. 
 
It all helped me design better marketing strategies that resonate with prospects.

Case Studies Are Your Greatest Marketing Weapons:

For tech service companies, case studies are like an elixir. 

We wrote over 50 case studies, creating them in all possible formats to share with various prospects.

They accelerated our sales conversion like anything.
