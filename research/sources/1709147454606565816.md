Web3 founders and marketers-

Here's a simple positioning exercise that can greatly assist you in improving on GTM and product market fit. 

Grab a doc and dive into these questions with unwavering focus:

1) What do we do? What problem do we solve? 
- Provide a concise and precise answer.  

2) In which market category do we compete?  

3) How is our approach to solving the problem the best? What sets us apart from the competition?  

4) What are our unique attributes or features? What key benefits do we offer? How do we differentiate ourselves from the competition? 
- Consider our approach, processes, or intellectual property.  

5) Who would be most interested in our product and solution? 

- Identify the characteristics of users who would be particularly excited about the value we provide. How can we identify them?  

6) If not us, what alternatives would users choose? What are the competitive alternatives available?
