Crypto marketers must prioritize a product marketing approach from the get-go.  

Here are the basics of product marketing to get started:

1) Acquisition

- Define your ideal anons / users. (+100 aura points if you write a full user profile.)

- Pick the channels: Twitter, Farcaster (mini-apps?), Telegram, Base App (eventually).

- Map your content loops and campaigns.

- Line up ecosystem alignment + integrations.

- Build a referral program 

2) Onboarding

- Nail the flow with the dev team.

- Decide what “onboarded” means: wallet connected, first tx, or something else.

- Engineer that first “aha” moment inside the product.

3) Engagement & Retention

- Acquisition is easy; retention is hard.

- What hero features keep people coming back? Points? New drops?

- How do you level users from casual to power?

- Segment by key onchain metrics, track why they churn, plan win-back plays.

- Set up analytics: GA for web, Redash + Dune for on-chain.

4) Monetization

- Spell out why, how, and what you’ll charge.

- Crypto loves “free,” but sustainable revenue > pure vibes. Think about this on day one.

Product marketing is really a stack of questions you answer early. 

Answers that turn straight into product execution.
