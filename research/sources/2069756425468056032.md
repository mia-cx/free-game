Don't be just a marketer in the sea of mediocre marketers. 

Level up your game.

A scrappy list:

> Learn the tenets of product marketing. 

Acquisition, onboarding, engagement, retention, monetization. You would be surprised to know how little most of the marketers understand about this. 

> If you don't have strong conviction about what you are marketing, that is the first battle you need to fight.

Go the extra length to understand who the enemy of your product or the brand is. Define that, define the messaging around it, and then build your distribution strategy.  

> Get fanatical about who your users are. Do everything you can to understand who you are marketing to. Message, have a call, have a conversation with whoever you think is your customer base.  

> Be outrageous in your marketing. As opinionated as possible. Mid messaging no longer works.  

> Master video as a communication channel. It doesn't matter which platform it goes on. It's important to understand what is the best way to capture the imagination of your audience through videos. 

> Storytelling is an overused word, and the advice around it is pretentious. It only works when you are actually offering a real and impactful change through your product. Your work as a marketer is to figure out what the focal point of that transformation is and convey it through a protagonist's POV.

> Product shelf life is now low, and retention is harder. Most of your time as a marketer should go into how you can retain.
