Ramp, a $60B company, increased its AI search visibility in accounts payable nearly 7x in JUST 1 month.

this is the genius AEO system behind it:

They identified a specific category where they were barely showing up

-> Ramp’s accounts payable AI visibility was just 3.2%

-> That gave them a clear category to focus on and a baseline to measure against

They studied the content AI was already citing

-> Automation, AI and software comparisons kept appearing

-> Only 6% of Ramp’s existing citations came from automation-related content

-> This revealed an opportunity to expand their coverage

They created pages for specific buying decisions

-> Accounts payable software for small businesses

-> Accounts payable software for large businesses

-> Two different buyers, with different requirements, getting dedicated answers

They supported those pages with related content

-> Accounts payable automation software comparisons

-> AI in accounts payable

-> Together, these pages covered more of the questions surrounding the purchase

the reported results:

-> The two targeted pages generated 300+ citations within one month

-> AI visibility increased from 3.2% to 22.2%

-> Ramp climbed from 19th to 8th among fintech brands in the accounts payable category

the lesson for your company:

your next AEO opportunity could be a buying question your website hasn’t answered properly yet.

start by asking:

-> Which questions should our product show up for?

-> Who gets recommended today?

-> Which pages are being cited?

-> Do we have a page that actually answers the full question?

then turn those gaps into specific experiments:

-> choose a buyer question

-> improve the relevant page or create one where coverage is missing

-> rerun the same prompts

-> measure citations and mentions

-> track whether those gains bring qualified visitors and customers

visibility gives you a signal. customers tell you whether it’s working.
