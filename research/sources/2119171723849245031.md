1/ how to get more SEO clicks from the pages you already have

dont write new articles. no backlinks to start.

just your search console data + claude, and the exact prompts i use

here's a reminder: AI is your writing assistant here, not your writer. it finds the gaps, and you fill them

2/ start with the pages google already likes

GSC → performance → pages
sort by clicks, then impressions
take your top 10. google already trusts them, so every fix here pays off faster than any new article would

3/ get the data into claude

two ways:
→ connect GSC through an MCP connector and claude pulls it directly
→ or export it: click a page → queries tab → export → upload the zip file in your claude

use the last 3 months, not 7 days. more data, clearer patterns.
do one page at a time or just get all pages at once.

4/ what you’re hunting for:

→ queries getting impressions that the article never mentions
→ queries sitting at position 10–30

positions 10–30 are page 2 and 3. 

google already thinks you’re relevant, your page just isn’t complete yet.
that’s the goldmine. doesnt hurt to build 1-2 backlinks for these pages

5/ prompt:

My site is [URL]. Here’s my GSC data for the last 3 months: [pull via connector or attached export]
Find my top 10 pages by clicks and impressions

For page #1, list queries with impressions that the article doesn’t directly answer or mention
List queries ranking in positions 10–30

For each one, tell me exactly where it belongs: new H2, new paragraph, FAQ entry, or a tweak to an existing sentence
Rewrite only the sections that need to change, don’t rewrite the whole article

Current article: [paste or URL]

Stop after page #1 and wait for me before moving to the next.

6/ fix the intro

answer the main question in the first 2 paragraphs.

no “in today’s digital world” intros. no 300 word story before the point.

readers decide in seconds if you have the answer. so does google.

7/ keyword placement

your main keyword should go into:
→ the title
→ the meta description
→ the first paragraph
sounds basic. check your top 10 pages, i bet half of them miss at least one.

8/ prompt:

Here’s my article: [paste]
Main keyword: [keyword]

Check if the main query is answered within the first 2 paragraphs. If not, rewrite the intro so it is

Check that the main keyword appears in the title, meta description and first paragraph
Give me 3 title options under 60 chars and 3 meta descriptions under 155 chars

Keep my tone of voice, no fluff, no filler words

9/ make it yours and unique

this is where most AI content dies. add:
→ screenshots of you actually doing the thing
→ images, tables, simple diagrams
→ your own data, results and opinions
→ an angle nobody in the top 10 covers

I insist you write these couple of sentences yourself with 0 AI intrusion

AI can’t screenshot your dashboard. that’s your edge.

10/ prompt:

Here’s my article: [paste]

Tell me where a screenshot, image, table or diagram would help the reader more than text. For each one, tell me exactly what to show and why.

Then tell me what the top 5 results for “[keyword]” all say the same way, and suggest 3 unique angles I could add from my own experience.

11/ internal linking
link your 10 updated pages to each other where it actually makes sense, and point newer posts into them.

prompt:

Here are my pages with their URLs and main keywords: [paste list]

Suggest internal links between them. For each one give me the source page, target page, the exact sentence where it fits, and the anchor text. Only suggest links a reader would actually click. Max 3–5 per article.

12/ reindex

GSC → URL inspection → request indexing for every updated page.
wait 7–14 days. pull the queries again. repeat.

this loop is where the clicks come from.

13/ then: backlinks

these 10 pages are your best link targets. they already rank, so a few links can push them from page 2 to page 1.

2–5 a month is enough. free ones count too: directories, podcast guest spots, partner pages, answering journalist requests.

14/recap:

top 10 pages by clicks + impressions
GSC data → claude (MCP or export)
add missing queries + positions 10–30
answer the main question in the first 2 paragraphs
keyword in title, meta description, first paragraph
screenshots, visuals, unique angles
internal links + 2–5 backlinks a month

reindex, wait, repeat

improving what already ranks is the fastest SEO win most people ignore. 

give it 1–3 months and watch the clicks go up.
