A lot of people asking "where do you buy these backlinks" - like there are marketplaces for it.

There are, but don't use them.
Reality is much more boring.

You go search on Google or ChatGPT.
Something you imagine your users would search for where you'd like your product to get recommended.

You see neither Google or ChatGPT recommends you, instead, it recommends all your competitors.

Click on the sources (both Google and ChatGPT shows them). You'll see they're all listicles (mostly).

Open them in the browser. Then ask your favorite agent to sniff up the emails from the ones running them.

The send them all an email.

Cut the bs and fake flatter though. Don't lead with stupid stuff like "I just saw your blogpost on xyz and it really stood out to me". Just get to the point and offer to pay.

Straight up. Tell them how you found them and say you'd like your product in there and that you're happy to pay. That's literally it.

Some will reject, some will happily take your money. Rinse and repeat.
