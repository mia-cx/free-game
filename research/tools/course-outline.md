# Course outline

The course is **Free Game**: marketing and growth learned from zero, rewritten from free posts on X. The reader is a builder (developer, designer, maker) with no marketing background who has ADHD.

Order teaches from scratch: what marketing is → writing → content and channels → launching and outreach → search → keeping users → agents → judgment and career. Foundations lesson 5 introduces the funnel; later modules fill in its stages.

Each lesson lists the knowledge units it covers, by batch letter and exact `###` heading in `research/analysis/<batch>.md`:
A = `a-business-content-video.md`, B = `b-x-growth.md`, C = `c-search.md`, D = `d-agents.md`, E = `e-fundamentals-outreach.md`.
Every unit appears in exactly one lesson; duplicates across batches are listed together and must be merged into one treatment.
