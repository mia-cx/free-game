title: Fit your strategy on one page
summary: Write a one-page strategy, a three-month plan and a weekly rhythm to run it.
---

> [!TLDR]
> - Plan every task in one order: goal, audience, creative, channel.
> - Keep the whole strategy on one page with four sections.
> - Plan three months at a time, and pair each outcome goal with inputs you control.
> - Run the work from a board, a calendar and a weekly review of the numbers.

## Decide in four steps, in order

Run every marketing task through the GACC brief: **goal, then audience, then creative, then channel**. The order matters. The channel comes last because you can't choose where to post until you know what the post is for and who should see it.

> [!TRICK] Write a GACC brief for every task
> - One brief per task, four headings: Goal, Audience, Creative, Channel.
> - Fill them in that order. Don't pick a channel until the first three are done.
> - In a new role, use it on day 2 to set three messaging pillars: "What we build", "Why now", "Who it's for".
> - Template and explanation from the MKT1 newsletter: https://newsletter.mkt1.co/p/the-gacc-marketing-brief-the-best

## Keep the strategy to one page

A strategy you can't read in one sitting won't get used. Keep it to one page with four sections. Your positioning doc from *Say what you are, for whom, against what* feeds the first two.

> [!TRICK] Fill in a one-page strategy
> - Four sections: Brand promise, Target audience, Growth strategy, Marketing initiatives.
> - The author's Notion template: https://crystal-molybdenum-2f8.notion.site/One-Page-Crypto-Marketing-Strategy-Template-cbfdf184d7064b319e6ba7264eb621cf
> - The template was written for crypto projects; the four sections work for any product.

## Plan three months at a time

Agree on objectives with anyone who has a stake, then write a three-month plan with goals you can actually hit. Split it into weekly actions.

Pair every outcome goal (a macro KPI) with input goals you control (micro KPIs). You can't force the outcome, but you can force the inputs.

| Type | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Macro KPI (outcome) | Improve bounce rate by 10% over 3 months |
| Micro KPI (input) | Publish 3 articles a month, each reaching a set number of visits |

Report weekly and monthly from Google Analytics and Search Console. If goal-setting is new to you, HubSpot's [SMART goal examples](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/smart-goal-examples) are a good start. This approach comes from one marketer's four years building a marketing function through to the company's acquisition.

## Run the week from a board and a calendar

The same marketer runs execution with three tools:

- a kanban board (Notion) for the big picture
- a calendar for daily tasks
- a weekly review of the data, where you double down on what worked

> [!DO] Fill in your one-page strategy
> Copy the template or open a blank page with the four headings: Brand promise, Target audience, Growth strategy, Marketing initiatives. Write two or three lines under each, and one macro KPI with one micro KPI for the next three months.

> [!CHECK] What are the four steps of a GACC marketing brief, in order?
> Goal, audience, creative, channel. You fill them in that order for every task.

> [!CHECK] Your three-month goal is to improve bounce rate by 10%. What goal do you pair with it?
> An input goal you control, such as publishing 3 articles a month. You can't force the outcome, but you can force the inputs.
