title: Shape posts for skimmers
summary: Give posts a clear shape and format them so phone readers can scan them.
---

> [!TLDR]
> - Pick a shape for every post, open with proof and close with a directed last line.
> - Most readers scan on a phone: short paragraphs, one thought per line.
> - Cut every word that doesn't change the meaning.

## Give every post a shape

A post without a structure reads like a ramble. Pick a shape before you write, then open fast and close firmly. **The last line is the second most important line** after the hook, so never let a post trail off.

> [!TRICK] Pick a shape, open fast, close firm
> Six shapes:
> - Problem → Insight → Takeaway
> - List: hook + numbered points + closer
> - Graphic
> - Video
> - Article
> - Quote post
>
> Open: cut filler, get to the point, and show proof and credibility as early as you can.
>
> Close with one of three: the takeaway in 1 to 2 lines, a question, or a tease of what's next.

## Format for a thumb, not a desk

Most people read on their phone, and they scan before they read. Format so a scanner can pick up the point.

> [!TRICK] Format for phone skimmers
> - Use line breaks. No walls of text.
> - Keep paragraphs to 1 to 3 lines. Four lines look like an essay on a phone.
> - One thought per line. A sentence with two "and"s and a comma is probably two sentences.
> - Mix short and long sentences.
> - Use numbers, lists, bullets and arrows.
> - Add connector lines that pull readers down: "Let me explain...", "But here's what nobody tells you:".
> - Preview the post on your phone before you publish.

## Make every word earn its place

Tight, plain words read faster and keep you from sounding machine-written. The test is simple: if deleting a word doesn't change the meaning, delete it.

> [!TRICK] Cut and sharpen word by word
> - Cut filler: really, very, just, actually.
> - Use simple words: utilise → use, facilitate → help, commence → start.
> - Use strong verbs: "grew really quickly" → "exploded".
> - Use active voice: "Mistakes were made" → "Most creators make this mistake".
> - Say "you" more than "I".
> - Avoid AI tells: em dashes, and the "it's not [X], it's [Y]" pattern.

> [!DO] Reformat one post
> Take your last post or a draft. Break it into lines of one thought each, cut every "really, very, just, actually", and rewrite the last line as a takeaway, a question or a tease. Preview it on your phone and save the new version.
