> [!TLDR]
> - A content map is a tree: 3 to 4 pillars, 5 to 7 topics each, 5 to 10 ideas per topic, 4 to 5 angles per idea.
> - Build it from your brand documents plus research on what has performed in the last 6 to 12 months.
> - Collect patterns like an artist collects references. Never copy a post 1:1.

## A content map is a tree

Most people sit down to post and start from a blank page. A content map means you never do: **you pick the next angle off a tree** you already built.

The shape Dan Koe uses:

| Level | How many | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pillars | 3 to 4 | Broad themes you'll own |
| Topics | 5 to 7 per pillar | Areas inside each theme |
| Ideas | 5 to 10 per topic | Specific points likely to perform |
| Angles | 4 to 5 per idea | Ways into the same idea |

Even at the low end that's 3 × 5 × 5 × 4 = 300 angles. Koe says one run gives about six months of ideas. That's unverified, but at 3 to 5 posts a week, the arithmetic leaves room to spare.

## Merge your lens with what already works

The map has two inputs. Your brand and voice documents (from *Write down what makes you you*) supply the pillars and the lens: how you see things differently. Research supplies the proof: which ideas and formats have actually performed in those topics over the past 6 to 12 months.

Either input alone fails. Pure lens gives you original posts nobody asked for. Pure research gives you the same posts as everyone else.

> [!TRICK] Generate the map with one prompt
> Attach your brand document and voice document, then run this. Eden is Koe's own research tool; swap in whatever research tool or web-enabled assistant you have.
>
> ```
> I want you to create a research backed content map: 3-4 unique and broad content pillars that branch into 5-7 topics that are both based on ideas that are working, through my own unique lens based on [@ brand document] and [@ content document]. Branching off each topic should be 5-10 ideas that are likely to perform well, with 4-5 angles branching off of each of those ideas.
>
> Look over my brand and content docs, pull potential pillars and my lens out of it, then use Eden to research what's performing across platforms in those topics. We want a unique content map, but we want to merge it with patterns that have been proven to perform over the past 6-12 months.
>
> Put the output in an easy to read document.
> ```

## Collect references, don't copy

Researching what performs can feel like selling out. Koe frames it as an artist's inspiration board: you pull patterns (a structure, a hook shape, a format), then fill them with your own ideas.

One way to stay original: research platforms you don't post on. Koe says that's where you find the ideas nobody on your platform has used yet. *Reuse formats that already work* goes deeper on copying the format and never the content.

> [!DO] Sketch the top of your map
> In `content-map.md`, write 3 pillars. Under the one you know best, write 5 topics. Run the full prompt later, once your brand document exists.
