> [!TLDR]
> - A post has about 48 hours to take off, and posting too often means some posts never get a chance.
> - Small accounts get a built-in boost on fresh posts. Big accounts get no guaranteed reach.
> - Your account has a reputation score built from who engages with you and how often you're blocked or reported.
> - Write your own posts, stay out of fights and check X's transparency tool for labels.

## Each post lives about 48 hours

X is real-time. Ryan (@ryangrowthh), reading X's open-source code, says posts older than about 48 hours are filtered out, so **a post makes or misses its reach within two days**.

The filter also drops posts the viewer has already seen, reposts and replies from accounts they don't follow, blocked and muted accounts and keywords, and spam, flagged and bot accounts. Posts from accounts the viewer doesn't follow get their score multiplied by a discount below 1.

Posting more is not free. If you post too often, X doesn't give every post a chance. Ryan gives no threshold; *Run a daily article routine* sets a rule for how much to post.

## Small accounts get a head start

Fresh posts from small accounts get a cold-start boost, so a new account can go viral early. Ryan's own first viral post came three days after starting a new account. Ryan reports 8,000+ followers and 4M+ impressions in 4 weeks, solo, and about 9,900 followers in a month. That's one person's result.

The flip side: big accounts get no guaranteed reach. Ryan has watched many big accounts fade over about 18 months. Starting small is not a handicap.

## Reputation compounds or caps every post

Your account carries a credibility score, worked out PageRank-style from who follows and engages with you. Engagement from well-connected accounts in your niche counts for more than a big follower count.

- Engage genuinely with people in your niche to build a dense, high-quality network.
- Engagement groups (accounts that agree to engage with each other's posts) no longer have the effect they once did.

> [!RISK] Labels limit every post
> A label for spam or inauthentic behaviour cuts the visibility of your account or individual posts. Too many blocks and reports relative to likes can trigger one, and inauthentic behaviour is tracked over time. Avoid content that gets you muted, blocked or reported, and check X's transparency tool, which shows labels on your account and on posts marked as spam.

> [!TRICK] Keep your account clean
> - Don't let AI write and post 100% of your copy. X tracks inauthentic behaviour over time.
> - Get Premium, and verify your ID if you're comfortable.
> - Avoid negative and political content.
> - Don't argue with trolls. Block or mute them. If they mute or block you, your reach drops.
>
> This is Ryan's advice from reading the code, unverified beyond that.

> [!DO] Check your labels
> Open X's transparency tool and look for labels on your account or posts. Write down what you find, even if it's "none".
