> [!TLDR]
> - Days 1 and 2: write down what you do and for whom, then turn it into positioning. No posting yet.
> - Days 3 and 4: post with what you have (screenshots, Loom demos) and anchor on the ecosystem you build in.
> - Days 5 to 7: show proof, ship something people can click, and recap the week in public.
> - After week one, build community in two phases: onboard and listen, then activate and reward.

## Days 1 and 2: get clear before you post

Kunal Gandhi wrote this plan for a marketer joining a day-0 crypto project, but nothing in it depends on crypto. It works for any product with no marketing yet, including your own.

**Day 1, clarity.** Before you touch X or design a banner, answer five questions in one doc. If you can't, you don't understand the product yet.

- What do we do?
- What problem do we solve?
- In which category do we compete?
- Why are we better?
- Who exactly are we for?

**Day 2, positioning.** Run the Goal → Audience → Creative → Channel plan from *Fit your strategy on one page*. Map three messaging pillars: "What we build", "Why now" and "Who it's for". Check them with the founders and developers, because marketing is not separate from the product.

## Days 3 and 4: post with what you have

**Day 3, first three posts.** Use screenshots, mockups or Loom demos. Don't wait for a designer. Show what you're building and what you're promising, and build anticipation.

> [!TRICK] The early-testers post
> "We're building X for Y. Early testers wanted."

**Day 4, ecosystem.** Anchor the launch on the platform or ecosystem you build in, and use your integrations as the onboarding story. Run a thunderclap: founders, friends and ecosystem people post together (see *Line up your launch-day voices*).

## Days 5 to 7: proof, something to touch, a recap

**Day 5, social proof.** Highlight every integration, quote replies from users, and show the feedback loop. Talk to people, not at them. Make early users feel seen.

**Day 6, ship something touchable.** A waitlist, a leaderboard or a beta quest: anything people can click or join. Execution over polish.

**Day 7, recap.** Write a weekly thread on what worked, what didn't and what's next. Tag the people who helped. Momentum is built in public.

## After week one

Build community in two phases: **onboard and listen**, then **activate and reward**. Use chat groups (Discord, Telegram) for depth and X for reach. Show the product at hackathons and partner showcases.

Keep the weekly recap thread going, track daily execution in one place, and review the numbers weekly to double down on what works (*Fit your strategy on one page* covers the weekly review).

The plan is the author's own playbook. The post was bookmarked 223 times, but it reports no outcomes, so treat it as a sensible order of work rather than a proven result.

> [!DO] Do day 1 now
> Open a doc and answer the five clarity questions in one sentence each. Leave any you can't answer highlighted: that's your first conversation with the founders or with yourself.
